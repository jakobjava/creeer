Rejected (G1.4c): after the coupled closing, the middle joint closing on and the last opening,
the tip kept on the handle, until the middle bone met it. Every bone met the handle (middle
gaps of 6-15 mm down to 0-2.8 mm), but the last joint opened to 0-11 degrees: a straight
fingertip. Inserted in Hand::wrapped, after the closing loop:

        // The tip met the handle before the middle bone: as a fingertip slides round a handle,
        // the middle joint goes on closing and the last opens, the tip kept on the handle, until
        // the middle bone bears on it too (in a power grip every phalanx does: Kong and Lowe
        // 2005).
        let (bend, open) = (MOST[1] / f64::from(CLOSING), MOST[2] / f64::from(CLOSING));
        while bends[1] + bend <= MOST[1] {
            let mut next = bends;
            next[1] += bend;
            while meets(&next, 2) && next[2] > open {
                next[2] -= open;
            }
            if meets(&next, 1) || meets(&next, 2) {
                break;
            }
            bends = next;
        }
