# ADR-0026: rejected experiments

The code of approaches measured and rejected in ADR-0026's "Measured and rejected" tables, kept so they can be read or re-run.

| File | What it is | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| `procedural-head.patch` | The engineer's second procedural blockout: the hood removed and a head shaped from a sphere (an ellipsoid of ANSUR's head measures, a nose, brow, eye sockets, lips and chin as soft rises and hollows, a hair shell, ears and eyes), with thinner garments. The user: "the face still looks very cursed"; replaced by Blender Studio's base mesh | `art/engineer/blockout.py` at commit `30a8fd8` |
| `diamond-quilting.py` | The coverall's diamond quilting: stitched lines about 8 cm apart crossing at right angles round each limb, the padding puffed between them, after insulated workwear (US patent 4,460,645). The user: "I hate the diamond shapes"; replaced by canvas with double layers over the knees and seat | A function of `art/engineer/detail.py` while the detail was made (G1.3f) |
| `light-beard.py` | A light beard on the jaw, chin, upper lip and sideburns, a shell in the `hair` token. The user: "The beard looks bad, remove it" | A function of `art/engineer/hair.py` while the hair was made (G1.3f) |
| `boots-of-points.py` | The boots grown as a ball round each of the skin's points (Blender's points to SDF grid): every toe showed as a lump; replaced by the foot closed into a solid and grown (`last.py`) | An edit script for `art/engineer/last.py` as it then was; it does not apply to the final file |
| `belt-held-weights.patch` | The jacket and coverall under the belt weighted as the trunk alone: the layers beneath still turned with the thighs and parted from them at the belt line (11.8 mm at the hips' limits) | `art/engineer/skinning.py` at the commit that adds it (G1.3f, the fit) |
| `weights-by-ray.patch` | Every garment weighted as the skin where a ray in along its normal meets it: the hips worse (11.9 mm), 34 of the coverall's points seen at a shoulder's limit | The same |
| `jacket-weights-by-face.patch` | The jacket weighted as the coverall's nearest face: the elbow's crook unchanged (10.7–11.0 mm), new pokes at the shoulders (7.3 mm) and hips (11.4 mm) | The same |
| `sleeves-above-wrist.patch` | The coverall's sleeves ending 1.5 cm above the wrists: the gloved hand bent fully still met the cuff (11 mm), and the forearm was seen at rest past the cuff's wider mouth | `art/engineer/outfit.py` at the same commit |
| `joint-experiments.py` | The experiments on the joints closed fully (2026-10-22): the close-up level built with nothing decimated (edge loops at their limit: the elbow 11.9 mm through), the thighs' weight faded over 4–10 cm, and every way of hiding what a wearable encloses that was tried (by one ray, by the face's middle, on its limb only, along its own normal, cones of 45–70°), each switched on by an environment variable | `art/engineer/` at commit `7428122`, patched as it runs |
| `crook-fold.py` | The measure that showed why edge loops could not part the elbow's layers: where dual-quaternion skinning folds the crook over itself (the turn's change along the arm times the distance from its axis past 1) | Any build's `target/art/engineer.blend` |
