# ADR-0043: A client's frame: what its prediction costs, and what it need not do

- **Status:** Accepted (G2.11 stream A5, 2026-11-14; `docs/playability-plan.md`).
- **Context:** the user: the game "plays horrible"; "I really want to keep this game as performant as possible". What a player feels is their own frame, and only the server's tick was gated. Measured in G2.10 (`play::client_probe`): a client's prediction took 44–50 ms a frame in *builders* within CI's 600 ticks (66 and 85 ms at full length), and a fastening made cost *log_bridge*'s clients 382 Newton iterations at once. A frame at 60 Hz is 16.7 ms for everything.
- **Builds on:** ADR-0008 (snapshots, prediction, reconciliation), ADR-0025 (the bodies near the avatar predicted with it; their choice by reach, G2.10), ADR-0021 (the clients' work measured and gated: `Tick::client_work`, `CLIENT_WORK`), ADR-0036 (the own load drawn by the server's law), ADR-0039 (the bridge site).
- **Evidence:** `creeer_server`'s `world/building_parts.rs` (`make_built`: a part made a structure told anew), `predicted/nudge.rs` (small mispredictions moved in place), `predicted/rewind.rs` (setting back, as before), `creeer_physics`' `structure/history.rs` (`NodeState::moved_to`); the probes `predicted/probe_tests.rs` (`client_frames`, `a_lone_avatar`); the bench `client/bridge_bench.rs` and its gate `client/bridge_bench_tests.rs`; `creeer_client --play-bench bridge` (the whole frame, drawn). Tests: `client/told_tests.rs`, `predicted/tests.rs`, `server/held_view_tests.rs`, `server/build_flow_tests.rs`, `server/build_play_tests.rs`, `play/tests.rs` (`CLIENT_WORK`).

## Measured first

The probe `client_frames` (`SCENE=<scene> TICKS=<n> cargo test --release -p creeer_server --lib client_frames -- --ignored --nocapture`) plays a scene in lockstep and takes, per client and tick: the prediction's Newton iterations and those of its replays; whether each snapshot agreed, was moved in place or was rewound, and why (the avatar off, a body off, the bodies chosen changed); by how much and in what (position, velocity, spin, orientation); the bodies predicted and how many were awake. What it found, in order of cost:

1. **A fastening made was never told to the clients.** Parts fastened become one structure under the first part's entity (`fasten`, `make_built`); a client learnt an entity's kind only when it came into view, or when a hand let go of it. Fastened while nobody held them (every play scene's builders; in the game, the part aimed at while the other is held) the clients went on drawing the structure as the lone rigid part it had been, at its node 0, and predicting it so: its partner gone, it fell where the partner had been and disagreed with every snapshot. *log_bridge*: 382 Newton iterations at the nails, then a rewind at every snapshot for two seconds (121 a tick on average over ticks 400–500); *builders*: the same at each of its sixteen nailings. The player saw the part jump half its length and its partner vanish.
2. **The avatar alone is never mispredicted** (`a_lone_avatar`: standing, walking, running, stopping, jumping every 3 s, lossless: no rewind but the two at joining). In contact it is: walking into a crate, 119 of 150 snapshots rewound. Of all rewinds in *builders*, *crowd* and *work*, nine in ten were for errors under 5 mm, the avatar off in velocity, spin or orientation by a little; each replayed every body near it.
3. **Bodies still on the server rattled on the client.** A body the server holds still (asleep, or awake below what its quanta show: *builders*' crossed poles, *log_bridge*'s stacked sills and plank) was set to the snapshot's quantised pose (up to half a millimetre and 20 µrad off where it rests), stepped from a reset history and moved by millimetres a second: the next snapshot disagreed again.
4. **What remains is the physics of what the avatar touches.** *builders*' worst tick (1,176 iterations at tick 211) is a pole kicked by its player's feet, spinning at 33 rad/s: 681 iterations in the input's own step, as on the server, and its replay on top.

## Prior work

| Source | What it does | For Creëer |
|---|---|---|
| Unreal Engine's networked physics (Chaos, 5.4 on: `FRewindData`, `NetworkPhysicsSettingsResimulation`) | Keeps every physics frame's state for rewinds; resimulates only once an object drifts past per-object thresholds of position, rotation, velocity and angular velocity, and within them applies a *runtime correction* towards the server's state without resimulating (`np2.Resim.RuntimeCorrectionEnabled`, `RuntimeVelocityCorrection`, stability multipliers) | Taken: small errors corrected in place, no replay (`nudge.rs`). Its kept frames measured and rejected (below) |
| Valve's Source engine, `cl_pred_optimize 2` | No re-prediction where the server's update shows no prediction error | ADR-0025 already does it; kept |
| Glenn Fiedler, "Networked Physics in Virtual Reality" (2018) | Cubes at rest jittered after quantisation (quantised positions just above a surface fall back; quantisation pushes cubes into each other); cured by quantising both sides, limiting depenetration speed, and forcing a cube to rest once it has not moved for 16 frames | Forcing bodies the server has still to rest on the client was measured and rejected (below). Quantising the server's own state is not open: the server's results may not change |
| Fiedler, "State Synchronization" (2015) | An at-rest flag per body; at-rest bodies cost no velocity bits | The snapshot's `asleep` flag (ADR-0008) |
| Photon Quantum, prediction culling | Entities outside a prediction area are not simulated in predicted frames, only in verified ones | ADR-0025's bubble by reach, as before |
| Unity Netcode for Entities (physics, prediction switching) | Interpolated ghosts are kinematic in the client's physics world; only predicted ghosts are simulated; a switch is smoothed | As ADR-0025; nothing further taken |

## Decision

1. **A part made a structure is told anew** (`make_built` marks its entity changed unless it is in someone's hands: every client is sent its despawn and its spawn as a structure; one in hands is told as the hands let go of it, as before). Two lines in stream B's `building_parts.rs`; the server's world and every play and stress hash are unchanged (`creeer_tools play --short --check`).
2. **Small mispredictions are moved in place on a good link** (`nudge.rs`): where every body that disagrees (the avatar's included) is off by at most 5 mm (its velocity's error carried over the inputs pending included), 5 mrad, 5 cm/s and 50 mrad/s, the bodies near the avatar are the same and at most 4 inputs are pending, each is moved by its difference as uniform motion (the prediction now, and the motions kept for the inputs since), its solver's history moved with it (`NodeState::moved_to`, a new reader of `creeer_physics`' history: no result of the physics changes), and nothing is replayed. The drawn bodies slide by those millimetres as corrections always did. What is lost against a replay, and what the player would notice: the contacts' answer to a difference of millimetres over the two or three inputs pending, which the next snapshot measures again; nothing to see. Over a slow link everything is replayed as before.
3. **Otherwise the prediction is set back as before** (ADR-0025): rebuilt from the snapshot, the inputs since replayed. Nothing the player can touch is interpolated instead of predicted: bumping into things is predicted as before.
4. **Measured and gated** (`client/bridge_bench.rs`): at the bridge site with its footbridge built (ADR-0039), one builder through her own connection walks among the spare timber, takes a handrail post, carries it before her eyes to the bridge's near end, holds it there and puts it down; her prediction's Newton iterations a tick are gated, worst and 99th percentile, lossless and over the slow link. `creeer_client --play-bench bridge` plays the same with the frame drawn, phase by phase. The scenes' `CLIENT_WORK` gates come down to what was reached.

## Validation

- **Told anew** (`client/told_tests.rs`, the user's case as the scenes make it): two poles crossed, nailed by a command while nobody holds them; the client has the survivor as a structure (it had the lone pole's box), the other gone, the structure drawn within a centimetre of where the server has it and not predicted as a lone body. Without the fix: `Discriminant(0)`, the rigid body.
- **Prediction quality, unchanged gates** (all pass): walking into crates over the eight placements, lossless and over 100–130 ms with 5 % lost (`predicted/tests.rs`); the stick held in first person (`held_view_tests.rs`: frame steps, glide and rest within their limits); over a slow lossy link the avatar rarely corrected (`play_tests.rs`); building as the player builds (`build_flow_tests.rs`, `build_play_tests.rs`).
- **Server unchanged:** every play scene's hash at CI's length checked (`creeer_tools play --short --check`).

*builders*' 1.14 m correction was a player's avatar standing where a fastened pole was drawn as the lone pole it had been. *log_bridge*'s 51 corrections over 1 cm are its builders holding still while nailing: the hands' pull on the body is not predicted (ADR-0036, Limits). The session's worst came walking among the timber in the first seconds, when this machine's hosted server ran behind and every snapshot's rewind replayed many inputs (1,665 inputs replayed in 190 rewinds over the run); from 9 s the hosted server took 0.2–1.7 s a tick beside the built bridge (stream A6), and the client's frames went on (session under 8 ms in most seconds) while its render took 15 ms at the median. The render is stream A1's; all of these times are to be measured again on a quiet machine.

## Limits (also in `docs/future-improvements.md`)

- The client steps what its avatar touches as the server does: a pole kicked by the feet costs the client its own step (681 Newton iterations in one, *builders*) and its replays. The cure is the physics' (stream A6), not the prediction's.
- A body in contact with the avatar is still mispredicted within a snapshot or two (contact is stiff, and the client has neither the server's friction anchors nor other players' bodies), and rewound when off by more than the in-place limits.
- Bodies the server holds still but awake still rattle on the client and are rewound (the forced rest above was rejected).
- Over a slow link every misprediction is replayed, as before.
